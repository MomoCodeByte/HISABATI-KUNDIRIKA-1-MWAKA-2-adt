Mamia yana mafungu tisa ya sarafu. Makumi yana mafungu tisa ya sarafu. Mamoja yana sarafu tisa. Hatua 1. Hesabu au taja mafungu yenye maelfu; unapata mafungu tisa yenye 1000 kila moja. Sawa na; 1000 + 1000 + 1000 + 1000 + 1000 + 1000 + 1000 + 1000 + 1000 = 9000. 2. Hesabu au taja mafungu ya mamia; unapata mafungu tisa yenye 100 kila moja. Hii ni sawa na; 100 + 100 + 100 + 100 + 100 + 100 + 100 + 100 + 100 = 900. 3. Hesabu au taja mafungu ya makumi; unapata mafungu tisa yenye sarafu 10 kila moja, ambazo ni; 10 + 10 + 10 + 10 + 10 + 10 + 10 + 10 + 10 = 90. 4.
